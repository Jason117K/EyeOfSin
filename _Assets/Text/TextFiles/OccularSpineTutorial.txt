Utilize [b]occular spines[/b] to block zombies, halting them in their tracks while they attempt to break through. 
 
[b]occular spines[/b] will regenerate health over time when not being damaged.
 
[b]occular spines[/b] cannot block flying enemies.
 
Click the [b]occular[/b] Spine Icon to select it and place it in a defensive position.
