Said to be inspired by the screams of his victims, the Maws are Argus’ most sinister creation. With an insatiable appetite, the chasmic throat of the beast appears to stretch endlessly into the depths of Syn’s core. - Can Insta-kill most enemies

- When a zombie is eaten, a digestion timer starts, and when the timer is done, the tentacle is free to consume another zombie
