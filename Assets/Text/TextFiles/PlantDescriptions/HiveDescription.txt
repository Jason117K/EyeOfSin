A Hive is made up of several layers of acidic wax layered over a mound of corpses. Once the barrier is established, Infernal Wasp eggs are planted that later hatch and feed upon the decomposed bodies inside. - Stalls incoming enemies 

- Spawns three wasps that orbit the demon, with each wasp attacking and stopping zombies that get close
- Wasps respawn a set time after they die, but when all wasps are dead, the Hive is defenseless

	- High Speed
	- High Numbers
	
- Weak VS
